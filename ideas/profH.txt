2. An inventory robot

https://fictionlab.pl/blog/what-is-a-robot-digital-twin-and-how-do-you-build-one-with-ros-2-and-gazebo/

Then when you start thinking through the "trash vs not trash" (in my mind anyway) you could pull from some of the processes used in factories to pull parts from a shelf and how they are training those system for object detection and identification:

https://oxmaint.com/industries/facility-management/ros-2-powered-digital-twin-for-facility-robotics
